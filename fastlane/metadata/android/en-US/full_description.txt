Cast audio from your Android phone to AirPlay 2 speakers — HomePod, HomePod mini, Sonos, Apple TV, Airport Express, and more.

Your Android phone cannot talk to AirPlay speakers. Google Cast and AirPlay are different ecosystems, and no phone bridges them. CastBridge does.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open CastBridge. Receivers on your Wi-Fi are discovered automatically.
2. Tap Start casting and confirm Android's capture prompt.
3. Play something in any audio app. The sound comes out of the speaker.

Everything runs on your phone. No bridge box, no Raspberry Pi, no computer to leave switched on.

CONTROLS
- Your phone's volume keys control the speaker while casting.
- One tap mutes the phone's own speaker, so you don't hear both.
- An ongoing notification keeps you in control; stop whenever you like.
- mDNS discovery finds receivers automatically, or type an IP address.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Everything stays on your local network. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no data collection. Your audio never leaves your Wi-Fi.
- Some apps (Netflix and other DRM-protected streaming apps) block audio capture by design. They play silently while casting; no app can capture them.
- Expect a couple of seconds of delay between phone and speaker. That is how the AirPlay protocol works, not a fault.
- Requires Android 10 or newer, an arm64 device, and the speaker on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Works with any app that permits playback capture: music players, podcast apps, browsers, video apps.
